Welcome to The Jody Parsley Legacy Organization!


Dear friends, family & fans of Jody Parsley,
please take a moment to read this page.

We ask for your help in archiving as much
information regarding Jody's life as possible.

Please reach out to legacy@jodyparsley.org


Jody Parsley is a musician whose story was largely ignored, never establishing a mainstream audience.

As a result, much of his history is sadly lost to time; many of his belongings have also been damaged or lost.

We hope to recover as much knowledge as possible whilst sharing his story with the world.

Many articles and much of the online content covering Jody have since been removed from the internet.

Any and all content we share will be provided publicly and permanently, at no cost ever.

Those who knew him well always testify that he was one of, if not the greatest men, they have ever known.

He clearly left a lasting impact on those around him, and he deserves to be heard around the world.

Jody was born June 19, 1927, and passed away on January 8, 2015, at the age of 87 after battling metastatic lung cancer.

Please be patient and considerate as we compile our findings and continue building this website in his honor.

- Brought to you by Baymon Blosser of The J.P. Legacy Organization.
